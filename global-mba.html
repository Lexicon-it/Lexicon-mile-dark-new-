ABOUT LEXICON MILE

Founded in 2009 as part of the Lexicon Group of Institutes (est. 2006), Lexicon MILE represents the Group’s commitment to building leaders who thrive in a borderless world. With a presence across preschools, K-12 schools, hospitality, media, fitness, and specialized training academies, the Lexicon ecosystem is a holistic platform for talent development.

At Lexicon MILE, our philosophy-One Student at a Time-has evolved into the global positioning of, “A Mentor Every MILE.” We invest deeply in the personal journey of each learner, combining academic rigor, mentorship from industry leaders, and global exposure to shape Day Zero Professionals-graduates who step into the workforce ready to lead from day one.

ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY OF SOUTH WALES, UK

The University of South Wales, UK, brings over 100 years of academic excellence to the partnership. Accredited by the Privy Council and assured by the Quality Assurance Agency (QAA), USW has earned its place among the UK’s most industry-focused universities.

Its global alumni community spans more than 120 countries, and its credentials in professional education, innovation, and applied research make it a natural partner for Lexicon MILE. Together, we deliver a Global MBA that combines Indian dynamism with UK academic heritage.

Global MBA Program Overview

The Global MBA at Lexicon MILE is designed for professionals who aspire to lead across borders, cultures, and industries. Offered in collaboration with international partner institutions, the program blends world-class academics with global internships, multicultural immersions, and career pathways into multinational organizations.

Students learn from international faculty, global industry leaders, and cross-cultural cohorts, equipping them with the skills to navigate complex economies and make strategic decisions in a borderless world.

At its heart, the Global MBA reflects our ethos: A Mentor Every MILE—ensuring learners are
guided not only by professors but also by seasoned global executives who have shaped
businesses across continents.

Key Highlights of the Global MBA Program

Elevate your career with the Global MBA (GMBA) program, a transformative two-year postgraduate qualification designed for aspiring business leaders. Delivered through a

combines international exposure, practical skills, and academic rigor to prepare you for global success. With a focus on real-world application, the GMBA equips you with the tools to thrive in dynamic business environments.

Admission process

At Lexicon MILE, we're committed to shaping the leaders of tomorrow. Our admission process goes beyond selection; it's a journey to understand the role you aspire to play in the world of business and leadership. Join us to embark on a transformative path!

Image Placeholder

Entry requirement Bachelors Degree in any field with a minimum aggregate of 55% &  Valid Passport

Criteria to Progress to Term 3 :

  • USW, UK applicable English Language Exam with requisite band/score in each component & overall.
  • Pass all the subjects of Term 1 and Term 2
  • IELTS overall score of 6 and no band below 5.5 on the scale of 9 or equivalent
  • Get a successful General student VISA (Tier 4) to study in the UK.
  • Please go through the UK Government website (https://www.gov.uk/tier-4-general-visa) to know more about VISA procedure and documents requirement.
    Note: You will be given assistance for VISA application during your first Term at Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership & Excellence.
