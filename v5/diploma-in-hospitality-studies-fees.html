Important Terms, Chef Uniform, Toolkit & Refund Guidelines

1. Part-A Industry Skills Modules: Participation in commercial kitchen simulations, F&B service labs, and hotel field workshops requires mandatory attendance (minimum 75%) and signed student-parent compliance undertaking submitted at reporting.

2. Uniform & Professional Tool-Kit Deductions: Each candidate receives tailored professional chef regalia, service uniform, safety shoes, and an imported culinary knife set. In the event of admission cancellation after uniform measurement or purchase order dispatch, actual uniform & tool-kit manufacturing costs are non-refundable.

3. Statutory Refund Guidelines: If a cancellation is requested prior to official program commencement, an administrative processing charge applies, and the balance of Part-A is refunded. Part-B University statutory fees are directly governed by YCMOU University refund statutes. All legal matters are subject to Pune jurisdiction.
