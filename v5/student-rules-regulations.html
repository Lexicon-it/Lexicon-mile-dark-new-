Student Rules & Regulations

Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence follows very stringent rules and regulations for the conduct of the student behaviour in and off Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence Campus.

Essential qualities like dress code, responsibility, discipline, time keeping, mannerisms etc. are highly emphasized upon as an integral part of the Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence culture.

Each student at Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence is expected to adhere to the rules, regulations, policies, practices and instructions set forth by the Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence Management for the Admissions, Curriculum, Student Leave Policy, Examinations, Hostel Conduct, Mess Conduct, Internships and Placements, from time to time and as mentioned in the Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence.

Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence does not support leave of students during an active trimester curriculum, other than the official allocated ones commonly applicable to all, unless sanctioned under special circumstances. Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence Management does not entertain any interventions / interferences from any family members/relatives/friends of students, in the regular course of PGDM program at Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence.

Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership and Excellence Management requests the cooperation of all concerned, to keep up to the sanctity of providing a transparent and quality based PGDM program, to all our students.
