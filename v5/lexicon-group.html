01 / GENESIS & GUIDING ETHOS

From 19 Students to an Educational & Enterprise Powerhouse.

Under the visionary leadership of Shri S. D. Sharma, alongside Mr. Pankaj Sharma (President) and Mr. Neeraj Sharma (Vice Chairman), The Lexicon Group was founded on the fundamental principle of “Believe in Yourself”.

What began as a single school in Wagholi, Pune has expanded exponentially. Driven by our guiding philosophy — “Day by Day, in every way, I am getting better & better” — our mission has evolved from pioneering child-centric K-12 education to cultivating industry-ready business leaders, hoteliers, animators, and empowering communities through fearless media, wellness, and accessible justice.

✦ Child-Centric Pedagogy

Fostering natural curiosity, creativity, and cognitive readiness from early childhood through higher education.

✦ Inclusive Empathy

Dedicated therapy and specialized curricula ensuring neurodivergent learners achieve full potential.

✦ Industry Synergy

Curricula co-designed with Fortune 500 executives and top hospitality chains for direct employability.

✦ Holistic Well-Being

Integrating physical fitness, civic awareness, and emotional resilience across every institution.
