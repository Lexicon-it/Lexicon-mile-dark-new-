Why This AI Lab Exists

Before tools and laptops, define the purpose. This AI Lab exists to transform PGDM students into AI-confident business leaders.

Our Mission

Solve Real Business Problems

Using AI to address actual challenges faced by businesses. Not hypothetical case studies, but live projects with measurable impact.

Make Students AI-Literate

Transform non-tech PGDM students into AI-confident professionals who can leverage AI tools for business advantage.

Produce Deployable Outputs

Build dashboards, bots, automations, and models that can be deployed in real business environments.

Revenue & Reputation Lab

Generate revenue through industry projects and certifications while building the institution's reputation for AI excellence.

This Is NOT a Data Science Degree Lab

This is an Applied AI for Business & Management Lab

The objective is managerial decision intelligence — not hardcore coding. We empower business leaders to leverage AI, not become software engineers.

Our Principles

Practice Over Theory

Every session produces tangible output. No passive listening, only active building.

Business Context First

AI tools are taught within the context of real business problems, not as abstract concepts.

Portfolio-Driven Learning

Students build a comprehensive AI portfolio that demonstrates capability to employers.

Industry Integration

Direct collaboration with businesses ensures relevance and creates placement opportunities.

Experience the Difference

Join a program where AI is practiced daily, not taught occasionally.
