Frequently Asked Questions.

Common questions regarding UK visas, credits, internships, degrees, and academic progression.

Which institution officially awards the MBA Global degree? +
Upon successful completion of the full program (60 credits at Lexicon MILE + 120 credits at USW), the degree is awarded directly by the University of South Wales, UK. USW is a recognized UK public university accredited under the Royal Privy Council and Quality Assurance Agency (QAA).
What is the visa success record for the UK component? +
Across 7 completed batches, Lexicon MILE has maintained a 0% visa rejection rate. Students receive step-by-step documentation guidance, bank balance verification assistance, interview mock trials, and official CAS letters directly from the University of South Wales.
Are healthcare services covered while studying in the UK? +
Yes. As part of the UK student visa process, students pay the National Health Service (NHS) immigration health surcharge, which grants them comprehensive free medical treatment under the British National Health Service during their stay in the UK.
What is the English language proficiency requirement? +
To progress to Term 3 in the UK, students must attain an IELTS Academic overall band of 6.0 with no component band below 5.5 (or an equivalent USW-recognized test). Intensive IELTS training and UK communication coaching are provided on campus during Term 1 at Lexicon MILE.
How does the 3–4 month UK internship work? +
During Term 4 in the UK, students undertake a mandatory 3 to 4 months corporate placement with companies in the UK or overseas, facilitated through USW's career network and our corporate partnership with RSR Global UK.
