The BBA Program at Lexicon MILE is designed to go beyond conventional business education by integrating core business principles with AI, analytics, and hands-on industry exposure.

ELIGIBILITY

01

Should be a citizen of India.

02

Should have passed the 10+2 examination with eligibility as per the admission policy of Savitribai Phule Pune University, Pune.

03

Students from any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) are eligible to apply.

04

Should have obtained a non-zero positive score in the Common University Entrance Test (CUET-UG) or a non-zero score in MAH-BCA/BBA/BMS/BBM CET 2026 conducted by the competent authority.

05

Candidates awaiting final results may also apply (subject to fulfillment of eligibility criteria).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the eligibility for BBA admission in Pune?

Students who have completed 10+2 (Science, Commerce, or Arts) from a recognized board are eligible to apply for BBA admission in Pune.

2. Is BBA a good course for career growth?

Yes, BBA provides a strong foundation in business, management, and leadership, opening career opportunities in corporate roles, entrepreneurship, and higher studies like MBA.

3. Does this BBA program include internships?

Yes, the program includes social and corporate internships, as well as live projects, to ensure real-world industry exposure.

4. What specializations are available in BBA?

Students can choose from Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Agri Business, and Services Management.

5. Does the program include AI and data analytics?

Yes, the BBA program integrates AI tools, data analytics, and industry platforms like Power BI and Tableau for practical learning.

6. What are the career opportunities after BBA?

Graduates can pursue roles in marketing, finance, HR, business analytics, or continue with higher studies like MBA, PGDM, or competitive exams.

7. Is BBA better than BCom for management careers?

BBA focuses more on management, leadership, and practical business skills, making it ideal for students aiming for corporate careers or entrepreneurship.

8. Are scholarships available for BBA students?

Yes, eligible students can apply for government scholarships as per applicable norms and guidelines.

9. What is the fee structure for BBA in Pune?

The total program fee is ₹5,70,000 (3 Years), with structured payment across academic years. Educational loan assistance is available through partner institutions, and eligible students can also apply for government scholarships as per applicable norms.
