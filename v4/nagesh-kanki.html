Professional Overview & Financial Pedagogy

Prof. Nagesh Kanki is an Assistant Professor of Finance at Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership & Excellence (Lexicon MILE), with 18 years of extensive academic experience in teaching, academic administration, examination management, and student development.

He is a highly qualified academician with an MBA in Finance & Marketing and an M.Com in Advanced Accounting & Taxation. He has also successfully cleared the UGC-NET in Management and SET in Commerce, demonstrating strong expertise across finance, accounting, commerce, and management education.

“Prof. Kanki brings together academic expertise, practical financial knowledge, and administrative leadership to create an engaging learning environment. His extensive experience enables him to mentor students effectively and prepare them to address real-world financial and business challenges.”

Teaching & Academic Expertise

Prof. Kanki has extensive experience teaching a wide range of finance and accounting subjects, combining strong theoretical foundations with practical applications, case-based learning, and industry-oriented examples:

Business & Cost Accounting

In-depth coverage of standard costing, variance analysis, marginal costing, inventory valuation, and core business ledger accounting practices.

Business Taxation & Practices

Direct and indirect taxation frameworks, corporate tax planning, GST compliance, and accounting and taxation practices for commercial enterprises.

Financial Statement Analysis

Ratio analysis, cash flow forecasting, Dupont analysis, and balance sheet diagnostics to evaluate corporate solvency, liquidity, and operational performance.

Management Accounting & Cases

Case-based teaching exploring real-world capital budgeting, working capital management, financial risk mitigation, and executive decision-making.

Academic Leadership, Administration & Institutional Governance

Over the course of his 18-year academic career, Prof. Kanki has contributed to several reputed institutions across Pune, including Sinhgad College of Commerce, ISB&M, Army Law College, and Kaveri College.

Alongside classroom teaching, Prof. Kanki has undertaken several important academic and administrative leadership responsibilities reflecting his strong involvement in academic governance and student engagement:

  • BBA Coordinator: Orchestrating undergraduate academic operations, curriculum sequencing, faculty alignment, and student mentoring.
  • College Examination Officer (CEO): Directing institutional examination logistics, evaluation standards, statutory university compliance, and confidential processing.
  • NSS Programme Officer: Spearheading National Service Scheme community outreach, leadership camps, and civic responsibility initiatives for students.
  • Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) Roles: Recognized SPPU Paper Setter and SPPU Paper Moderator for commerce and management curricula.
  • Curriculum Delivery & Assessment: Proven track record in curriculum design, outcome-based education, continuous evaluation, and institutional accreditation.
