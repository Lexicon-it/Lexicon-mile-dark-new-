COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about admissions, curriculum, hotel internships, and career scope.

The program is a 1-Year (11 months) fast-track diploma. It comprises 5 months of rigorous on-campus theoretical and hands-on laboratory training (culinary arts, food service, front office, housekeeping) followed by a 6-month embedded industrial internship at a 5-star luxury hotel.

Yes. The program is affiliated with Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University (YCMOU – Study Center No. 62582 · Program Code: V121), which is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), and accredited with NAAC ‘A’ Grade.

Candidates must have passed 12th standard (HSC / 10+2) from ICSE, CBSE, State Board or any recognized equivalent board in any stream (Arts, Commerce, Science). An equivalence certificate is needed if the board issues grades instead of percentage marks.

Students are placed across our partner 5-star luxury properties (such as Marriott, The Ritz-Carlton, Taj, Conrad, Hyatt Regency) for full-time operational rotation. Under the Earn-While-You-Learn framework, students receive a monthly stipend as per hotel property norms and often receive Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).

The total course fee is approximately ₹1,50,000. It is divided into Part-A (Institute Training Fees: ₹1,06,692 payable directly to the institute's verified Kotak Mahindra Bank account) and Part-B (University Examination & Registration Fees: ₹43,308 payable directly to YCMOU).

Graduates step into roles such as Commis Chef, Culinary Associate, Food & Beverage Executive, Guest Service Associate, Front Desk Officer, Housekeeping Supervisor, and Airline/Cruise Hospitality Crew across 5-star hotels, luxury cruise liners, and premium dining establishments.
