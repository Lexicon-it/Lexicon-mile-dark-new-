Industry Overview

Hospitality Industry

The hospitality industry is a multibillion-dollar sector with three core areas: Accommodation, Food and Beverage, and Travel and Tourism.

ACCOMMODATION

Includes hotels, motels, wellness centres, and other lodging businesses.

FOOD & BEVERAGE

Covers restaurants, fast food chains, and other establishments offering food and drinks.

TRAVEL & TOURISM

Encompasses airlines, trains, and cruise ships for Hospitality Management.
