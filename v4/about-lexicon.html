02 / FOUNDATION & GROUP REACH

The Lexicon Story.

A multi-disciplinary educational conglomerate committed to empowering individuals at every milestone of life.

THE CONGLOMERATE · ESTD. 2006

About the Lexicon Family.

Founded in 2006 in Pune, The Lexicon Group has evolved into a premier educational and business leader. From progressive K-12 schools and early childhood centers to higher institutes specializing in management, hospitality, media, animation, and a specialized therapy center for specially-abled students, we deliver transformative services across generations.

Beyond education, The Lexicon Group has successfully diversified into corporate research, news media (Pune Times Mirror, Civic Mirror), legal education, fitness (MultiFit), and wellness—governed always by an unwavering focus on quality, ethics, and societal upliftment.

Estd. 2006 15+ Group Entities Schools, Higher Ed & Special Needs Media & Wellness Conglomerate

FLAGSHIP BUSINESS SCHOOL · ESTD. 2009

About Lexicon MILE.

Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership & Excellence (Lexicon MILE), Pune, founded in 2009 as part of The Lexicon Group, has been transforming higher management education for the past 15 years and ranks among the premier management institutions in Western India.

Our two-year, full-time AICTE-approved Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) program transcends conventional textbook learning. With an agile start-up incubation cell, 9-in-1 industry certifications, and international university pathways, we cultivate versatile leaders ready to thrive from Day Zero.
