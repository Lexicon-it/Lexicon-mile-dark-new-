Lexicon Group

The Lexicon Group is a prominent Pune-based conglomerate founded in 2006, dedicated to fostering excellence across education, wellness, media, and innovative ventures. Headquartered in Pune, India, the group has evolved from its roots in education to a diverse ecosystem that impacts thousands of lives through quality-driven initiatives in multiple sectors. With a strong emphasis on self-belief, integrity, and community contribution, Lexicon Group serves over 16,000 students and employs hundreds across its operations, creating a holistic environment for growth and innovation. All its subsidiaries are primarily based or originated in Pune, reflecting the group’s deep ties to the city as a hub for educational and entrepreneurial advancement.

Our History

Lexicon Group was established in 2006 by visionary educator Shri S. D. Sharma, initially transitioning from a publishing background to focus on accessible, high-quality education in Pune. Starting modestly with just 19 students in its first year, the group faced early challenges like financial constraints but grew rapidly through persistence and a commitment to excellence. By building a sustainable educational ecosystem; including pre-schools, K-12 schools, and higher education institutes, the group expanded to over 10,000 students in Pune, cultivating a community of more than 20,000 students and parents. Over the years, diversification into wellness, edtech, media, and consumer products occurred organically, often through acquisitions and partnerships with passionate entrepreneurs, while maintaining a Pune-centric foundation. Today, Lexicon Group stands as a testament to turning ideas into impactful realities, with plans for pan-India and international expansion, including the establishment of The Lexicon University.

Our Subsidiaries in Pune

Lexicon Group’s subsidiaries are deeply integrated into Pune’s landscape, with operations spanning education, media, wellness, and beyond. Here’s an overview of key entities:

Lexicon Management Institute of Leadership & Excellence (Lexicon MILE)

Established in 2009 in Wagholi, Pune, this top-ranked MBA college offers AICTE-approved PGDM programs in areas like Marketing, Finance, Research & Business Analytics, and more. Focused on entrepreneurship and leadership, it boasts impressive placements with an average package of 8.25 LPA and a highest of 19 LPA, along with awards like Excellence in HR Leadership 2024. The institute features an Innovation & Incubation Centre to nurture startups.

The Lexicon Schools

Among the best CBSE schools in Pune, these institutions emphasize child-centric, progressive, and global education, fostering self-belief, ethical leadership, and innovation. Locations include The Lexicon International School (Wagholi and Kalyani Nagar) and The Lexicon School (Hadapsar), all in Pune.
