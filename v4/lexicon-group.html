Lexicon Group.

A prominent Pune-based conglomerate dedicated to fostering excellence across education, wellness, media, and innovative ventures, impacting thousands of lives through quality-driven initiatives.

WHO WE ARE

Empowering Lives Since 2006

The Lexicon Group has evolved from its roots in education to a diverse ecosystem. With a strong emphasis on self-belief, integrity, and community contribution, the Lexicon Group serves over 16,000 students and employs hundreds across its operations.

All its subsidiaries are primarily based or originated in Pune, reflecting the group’s deep ties to the city as a hub for educational and entrepreneurial advancement, creating a holistic environment for growth and innovation.

OUR HISTORY

From 19 Students to a Global Vision

Established by visionary educator Shri S. D. Sharma, Lexicon Group started modestly with just 19 students. Through persistence and a commitment to excellence, we built a sustainable educational ecosystem; including pre-schools, K-12 schools, and higher education institutes, expanding to over 10,000 students in Pune.

Over the years, diversification into wellness, edtech, media, and consumer products occurred organically. Today, Lexicon Group stands as a testament to turning ideas into impactful realities, with plans for pan-India and international expansion, including the establishment of The Lexicon University.
