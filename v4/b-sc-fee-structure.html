Important Guidelines (HMCT)

  • Module Participation: Part-A covers specialized employability and culinary skill modules. Participation requires online consent and an affidavit at the time of reporting.
  • University Portal: University fees are paid directly to YCMOU. Amounts shown reflect current approved schedules and are subject to official university revisions.
  • Uniform & Tool-Kit: Professional tailored uniform and international culinary kit are arranged post measurement. Deductions apply on cancellation after measurement/PO (₹15,000 for uniform and ₹3,000 for kit).
  • Jurisdiction: Pune jurisdiction applies to all disputes and academic transactions.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Part-A (Department Fees): Written cancellation requests must be submitted via email or in person. A nominal administrative charge of ₹5,000 is non-refundable from registration fees if cancelled prior to program commencement.
  • Part-B (University Fees): All university fee refund requests are processed directly by YCMOU in accordance with statutory state university refund norms.
  • Processing Window: Approved institutional refunds are processed and credited within 7–10 working days.
  • Disputes: Governing body resolutions and Pune legal jurisdiction apply.
