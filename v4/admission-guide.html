APPLICATION DOSSIER PREPARATION

Documents Required for Verification.

Keep clear, scanned copies (PDF or JPG format, maximum 2 MB per file) ready on your device prior to initiating the online application form.

Category 01

Academic Credentials

  • 10th Standard (SSC) Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • 12th Standard (HSC) Marksheet & Passing Certificate
  • Graduation Marksheets (Semester-wise or Consolidated transcript)
  • Provisional Degree Certificate or Bonafide letter from Principal (if in final year)
Required for: Eligibility Verification Format: PDF / JPEG
Category 02

Entrance & Identity

  • National Entrance Scorecard (CAT / XAT / CMAT / MAT / GMAT / ATMA / MH-CET)
  • Government Photo Identity (Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID)
  • PAN Card Copy (Mandatory for fee invoicing & bank loan sanction)
  • Category / Caste Certificate (With Non-Creamy Layer, if applicable)
Required for: Merit & ID Proof Format: PDF / JPEG
Category 03

Personal & Professional

  • 4 Recent Passport Photos (Color photographs with white background)
  • Updated Resume / CV highlighting leadership & project achievements
  • Work Experience Certificates & salary slips (if possessing >6 months experience)
  • Transfer & Migration Certificates (Required at the time of final campus reporting)
Required for: Profile Evaluation Format: PDF / JPEG

STATUTORY STANDARDS & EVALUATION CRITERIA

Eligibility & Selection Weightage.

Admissions at Lexicon MILE follow an equitable, transparent composite scoring model evaluating academic consistency, national entrance percentiles, personal interview poise, and professional potential.

Minimum Eligibility Benchmarks

PGDM Dual Specialization (2-Year Full-Time)

Bachelor's degree of minimum 3-year duration from any recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories). Candidates appearing for their final year bachelor degree examinations in 2026 are also eligible to apply provisionally.

MBA Global Programme

Recognized graduation degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks, coupled with English language proficiency assessment for global international transfer pathways with University of South Wales (UK) or INTI International University (Malaysia).

Accepted National Entrance Tests

Scores in any of the following national exams are accepted: CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT, ATMA, or Maharashtra MBA-CET. Scores must be valid for the 2026–28 academic admission cycle.

Composite Selection Weightage

Every candidate is evaluated holistically across four primary dimensions:

Entrance Test Score (CAT/CMAT/MAT/XAT/CET) 35%
Personal Interview (PI) & Presentation 30%
Past Academic Record (10th, 12th & Graduation) 20%
Work Experience & Extracurricular Diversity 15%
Total Composite Index 100% Score
