Online Grievance Mechanism

Faculty, Staff, and Students can write to the Chairman of the Grievance Redressal Committee (GRC) and Student Grievance Redressal Committee (SGRC) respectively in confidence for redressal of their grievances. Lexicon MILE believes in transparency and is committed to resolving any academic or non-academic grievances.
