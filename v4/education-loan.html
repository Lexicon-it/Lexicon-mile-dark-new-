TAX ADVANTAGE · INCOME TAX ACT 1961

Save Up to 30% with Section 80E Tax Deduction.

Under Section 80E of the Indian Income Tax Act, the interest paid on an education loan taken for higher education is 100% deductible from your gross taxable income. There is no upper monetary limit on the deduction amount, maximizing your financial savings throughout early career milestones.

No upper limit on interest deduction
Valid for up to 8 continuous assessment years
Available to Student or Parent Co-Borrower
Applicable on both Indian & Global MBA
8 Years
Consecutive Tax Deduction Window

Deduction begins from the financial year in which you start paying interest and continues for 7 subsequent assessment years.
