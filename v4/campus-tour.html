The campus is strategically located right on the Pune-Ahmednagar Highway (Gate No. 726, Wagholi). It is approximately 15 km (25 minutes) from Pune International Airport and 18 km (35 minutes) from Pune Junction Railway Station. App-based cabs (Uber, Ola), auto-rickshaws, and PMPML public buses operate frequently along this route.

Student safety is paramount. The campus is secured 24/7 with biometric access controls, professional security guards, and extensive CCTV coverage. On-campus first-aid facilities, tie-ups with leading multi-specialty hospitals in Wagholi, and round-the-clock emergency transport are always active.
