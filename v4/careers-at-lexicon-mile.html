TRANSPARENT RECRUITMENT

How Our Hiring Process Works.

Our collaborative four-stage selection process ensures alignment with Lexicon MILE's institutional values and educational excellence.

01

Profile Scrutiny

Our Board of Studies and HR team review your academic record, publication index, or corporate pedigree.

02

Initial Dialogue

A virtual or on-campus discussion on your research passions, teaching philosophy, and career objectives.

03

Teaching Demo

For faculty positions: interactive simulation with faculty peers and a presentation before our Academic Council.

04

Offer & Onboarding

Competitive offer issuance, relocation grant disbursement, and structured academic orientation.
