IN-PERSON EXPERIENCES

Campus Visit Guidelines.

Visiting our campus allows applicants, parents, and recruiters to inspect our infrastructure, lecture amphitheaters, and meet senior faculty:

Visiting Hours

Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM.
Campus counseling appointments are scheduled hourly. The campus is closed for prospective visitors on Sundays and public holidays.

Security & Gate Entry

All visitors must register at Gate No. 1 Security Checkpoint. Please carry a valid government-issued photo identity card (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN, or Voter ID) for gate entry passes.

Guided Tour Booking

Complimentary guided campus tours are led by admission ambassadors. Tours cover the AI Innovation Lab, classrooms, central library, and student cafeteria. Prior scheduling is recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about contacting our admissions team, reaching the Wagholi campus, and scheduling corporate visits:

Lexicon MILE is located along Pune-Nagar Road in Wagholi. From Pune International Airport (Lohegaon), the campus is approximately 10 km (about 20 minutes by app-based cab or auto). From Pune Junction Railway Station, the distance is roughly 14 km (35 minutes). Pre-paid taxis and auto-rickshaws are readily available 24/7 at both transit points.

For PGDM and Global MBA admission inquiries, you can reach out directly to our admissions counseling team on +91 72768 16262, +91 89560 59559, or +91 99674 27278. You can also send an email to admissions@mile.education or connect on WhatsApp for instant assistance.

While walk-in visitors are welcome between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM Monday through Saturday, we strongly recommend pre-booking your visit through our inquiry form or by calling our hotline. Pre-booking ensures an admission counselor and faculty mentor are reserved exclusively for your personalized tour and counseling session.

Corporate partners and HR executives interested in recruiting PGDM or Global MBA students for final placements, 9-month winter internships, or live consultancy projects should contact the Corporate Relations Cell directly at placements@mile.education or call +91 99674 27278. Our team provides fast placement broking and schedules recruitment drives.
