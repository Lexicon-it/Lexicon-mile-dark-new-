APPLICATION WORKFLOW

How to Avail Your Scholarship in 4 Steps.

Follow these four simple steps from application submission to fee deduction to secure your merit scholarship grant.

Step 01

Submit Scorecard

Enter your CAT, CMAT, or MAT roll number and upload your authentic entrance test scorecard during online registration on the portal.

Step 02

Clear PI & Selection

Appear for the Personal Interview (PI) round and secure your provisional PGDM admission offer letter from the admissions committee.

Step 03

Confirm Admission

Pay the first installment of fees within the stipulated offer window to lock in your scholarship allocation on a first-come, first-served basis.

Step 04

Fee Waiver Credit

Your verified scholarship grant is officially adjusted against the PGDM tuition fee installments, reducing your overall program cost.
