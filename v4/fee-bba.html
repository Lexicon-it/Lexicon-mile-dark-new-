Admission Cancellation & Refund Policy

  • Hostel & Examination Fees: Fees exclude hostel & mess; university examination fees applicable as per university norms.
  • AICTE & SPPU Guidelines: Refund (FRA-approved fees) will follow the guidelines of the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) & Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU).
  • Institutional Components: Other fee components will be governed by the institute’s internal policy.
  • Cancellation Timelines: Cancellation must be submitted in writing (via email or in person) within the prescribed institutional timelines.
  • Jurisdiction: All legal disputes are strictly subject to Pune jurisdiction.
