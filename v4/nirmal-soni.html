Professional Profile & Educational Philosophy

Dr. Nirmal Chandrakant Soni is an accomplished Academician, Scholar, and Advocate of Social Change with a distinguished career spanning over two decades in higher education. His academic interests lie at the intersection of Management, Development Studies, Environmental Studies, and Global Development.

With a teaching philosophy rooted in intellectual rigor, practical application, and innovative pedagogy, Dr. Soni strives to make complex management concepts accessible, engaging, and relevant to real-world challenges. His approach combines methodological precision with practical insights, enabling students to develop both conceptual understanding and professional competence.

“Through his academic leadership, research, mentoring, and social engagement, Dr. Soni continues to inspire learners and professionals to approach management not merely as a discipline, but as a powerful tool for responsible leadership and positive social change.”

Currently serving as Principal & Professor at the Institute of Leadership and Management, Pune (Affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University - SPPU), Dr. Soni steers curriculum design, academic governance, and interdisciplinary research while mentoring emerging managers to lead with integrity, ethical foresight, and sustainable strategic vision.

Core Academic Domains & Pedagogical Pillars

Dr. Soni champions an interdisciplinary academic framework integrating management theory, economic governance, and sustainable societal stewardship:

Management & Development Studies

Investigating the convergence of organizational management, economic administration, socio-economic policy, and global development paradigms to build robust institutions.

Environmental Studies & Externalities

In-depth research on aesthetic pollution, environmental degradation, negative commercial externalities, and sustainable policy frameworks for business ecosystems.

Methodological Precision & Pedagogy

Bridging academic rigor with real-world case simulations, innovative pedagogical models, and experiential learning so students master both theory and execution.

Behavioural Leadership & Coaching

Empowering scholars with personal mastery, ethical accountability, and transformative self-leadership drawn from executive life coaching methodologies.

Academic Credentials & Scholarly Research

Dr. Soni holds rigorous academic qualifications and professional accreditations spanning prestigious universities and premier national institutions:

  • Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.): Awarded by the renowned School of Social Sciences, Gujarat University, focusing on development economics, policy, and social sciences.
  • Master’s Degree in Management & Economic Administration: Conferred by Maharshi Dayanand Saraswati University (MDSU), Ajmer, establishing deep foundations in macroeconomic administration and corporate governance.
  • Company Secretaryship Qualifications: Successfully completed the Foundation and Intermediate levels of the Company Secretaryship programme conducted by the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).
  • National Eligibility Accreditation: Qualified the UGC-GSET, verifying highest national standards in university teaching and academic scholarship.
  • Scholarly Research & Publications: Research primarily investigates Development and Environmental Studies, specifically the socioeconomic implications of aesthetic pollution and negative externalities. He has presented his peer-reviewed research across national and international academic conferences and published widely in scholarly journals.

Career Journey, Professional Associations & Civic Impact

Beginning his academic career in 2000, Dr. Soni has held distinguished academic and administrative leadership roles across top-tier management institutions for over twenty-four years:

  • Balaji Institute of International Business (BIIB, Sri Balaji University, Pune): Served as Associate Professor, teaching international business management, research methodologies, and development economics.
  • Principal & Professor: Serving at the Institute of Leadership and Management, Pune, affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), guiding academic programs, faculty development, and university examination governance.
  • Indo Global Chambers of Commerce, Industry and Agriculture (IGCCIA): Serving as an Honorary Member, actively contributing to industry-academia linkages, trade dialogues, and regional economic development.
  • International Commerce Association: Proud Life Member, collaborating with global academics to foster high-impact commerce and trade research.
  • Civic & Road Safety Advocacy: Associated with an NGO dedicated to traffic safety, civic responsibility, and public awareness, instilling community consciousness in students and citizens alike.
  • Behavioural Development & Life Coach: Contributed as a Life Coach with Landmark Education’s Breakthrough Technologies, spearheading human-potential and behavioral-transformation initiatives.
