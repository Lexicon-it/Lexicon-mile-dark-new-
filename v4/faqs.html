Fees & Scholarships

Programme fees, payment schedules, and merit scholarship details

The total tuition fee for the AI-Integrated PGDM program at Lexicon MILE is approximately ₹10.8 lakh, depending on specialization and additional components. The fee is structured across milestone-based installments over the 2-year programme duration.
The MBA Global program follows a 60 + 120 credit international pathway in academic partnership with the University of South Wales (UK). The fee structure includes:
  • Registration Fee: ₹50,000 (non-refundable)
  • Year 1 (India): Tuition at Lexicon MILE covering 60 credits
  • Year 2 (UK): Tuition at USW covering 120 credits with UK living costs
Students may also avail education loan partnerships with SBI, HDFC Credila, Axis Bank, and Bank of Baroda.
Yes, Lexicon MILE offers merit-based and need-based scholarships for eligible PGDM students based on entrance exam performance. Scholarships are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to admission confirmation and payment of the first installment.
Students scoring 80 percentile and above in CAT are eligible for a scholarship of ₹1,00,000.
CMAT candidates with 90 percentile and above can receive ₹75,000, while MAT candidates with 90 percentile and above can receive ₹50,000.
Second-year scholarship continuation depends on the student's academic performance in the first year. The institute reserves the right to modify scholarship criteria or fee structures without prior notice.

Admissions & Eligibility

Application process, entrance exams, and eligibility criteria

Lexicon MILE accepts national-level entrance exams, including CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, MH-CET, ATMA, and GMAT. Candidates can submit their best score during Phase 3 of the application.
Candidates must have a minimum of 50% marks in Graduation from a recognized university along with a valid entrance exam score. Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview.
Yes, shortlisted candidates are required to appear for a Personal Interview as part of the final selection process. The candidate evaluation framework consists of: National Entrance Score (35%), Personal Interview & Case Study (30%), Past Academic Consistency (20%), and Work Experience & Leadership (15%).
Candidates can apply online through the official Lexicon MILE admissions portal by completing three simple phases:
  • Phase 1: Register your profile with personal details and programme preference
  • Phase 2: Pay the non-refundable ₹1,000 application fee online
  • Phase 3: Submit academic details, entrance test scores, and book your interview venue
Yes, admissions are currently open for the MBA Global program offered in academic partnership with the University of South Wales (UK). Apply through the official admissions portal.
