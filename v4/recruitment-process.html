CAMPUS ENGAGEMENT ROADMAP · 6 SYSTEMATIC STEPS

The 6-Stage Recruitment Lifecycle.

A streamlined end-to-end model from talent alignment to post-offer custom readiness, ensuring zero friction for corporate partners.

Stage 01 of 06 Initiation · Alignment

Recruiter Response Form (RRF)

The campus hiring cycle commences when our Corporate Relations office shares the official Recruiter Response Form (RRF). Organizations define their specific talent parameters, competency benchmarks, and compensation structure.

Submission of Job Descriptions (JDs), role specifications, and designated work locations.
CTC structure details including fixed component, incentives, and retention policies.
Eligibility benchmarks, program specialization preferences, and technical prerequisites.
Initiation Desk STAGE 01

Corporate Intake & RRF Alignment

Talent parameters, JD profiles, cut-off criteria & compensation structures officially registered.

Job Description Spec CTC Breakdown Specialization Mapping
Stage 02 of 06 Scheduling · Logistics

Campus Recruitment Scheduling

Upon receipt of the RRF, the placement team establishes mutual calendar alignment with the recruiter's Talent Acquisition team, reserving dates and facilities across in-person, virtual, or blended evaluation formats.

Finalization of preferred drive mode: In-Person Campus Drive, Virtual, or Hybrid.
Allocation of executive interview boardrooms, computer labs, and auditorium suites.
Verified candidate registration and profile mapping from the central placement portal.
Logistics Sync STAGE 02

Drive Scheduling & Facility Allocation

Calendar synchronization across in-person campus drives, virtual rounds, and hybrid slots.

Calendar Reservation Interview Suites Candidate Slotting
Stage 03 of 06 Presentation · Engagement

Pre-Placement Talk (PPT)

Corporate leaders address the graduating cohort in a high-tech auditorium setting, presenting organizational vision, business achievements, work culture, and future career advancement pathways.

In-depth presentation of company culture, operational verticals, and leadership values.
Role clarity regarding day-to-day responsibilities, KPIs, and reporting hierarchies.
Interactive open floor Q&A allowing students and panel to align expectations early.
Engagement Forum STAGE 03

Pre-Placement Talk (PPT) Keynote

Corporate leadership presentations, culture previews, career pathways & interactive Q&A.

300+ Seater Hall Vision & Values Interactive Q&A
Stage 04 of 06 Assessment · Interviews

Selection Process (Tests & PI)

Recruiters conduct comprehensive evaluations supported by student coordinators and dedicated test labs, ensuring rapid candidate flow and complete adherence to corporate assessment protocols.

Proctored online aptitude assessments, psychometrics, and technical domain evaluations.
Group Discussions (GD) assessing business communication, logic, and teamwork.
One-on-one technical interviews and senior management Personal Interview (PI) rounds.
Evaluation Funnel STAGE 04

Rigorous Multi-Tier Assessment

Online aptitude test labs, case-based Group Discussions & executive interview boardrooms.

Online Test Labs GD Moderation Technical Panels
Stage 05 of 06 Results · Confirmation

Final Offer Rollout

Upon conclusion of interview rounds, the hiring panel declares selected students. Our placement cell oversees prompt communication, Letters of Intent (LOI) distribution, and binding acceptance tracking.

Prompt publication of final selections and distribution of formal offer documentation.
Institutional policy-backed candidate acceptance and role reservation tracking.
Pre-onboarding documentation support, background verification, and joining alignment.
Offer Clearance STAGE 05

Official Offer Rollout & Acceptance

Swift publication of results, Letters of Intent (LOI) distribution, and joining alignment.

Formal LOI Release Placement Cell Guarantee Onboarding Verification
Stage 06 of 06 Pre-Boarding · Custom Readiness

Company-Specific Training & Readiness

Our partnership continues post-selection. Lexicon MILE collaborates with employers to deliver custom pre-boarding bootcamps, training recruits on proprietary tools, ERPs, and workflows so they contribute on Day Zero.

Targeted workshops on employer software suites, analytical models, and workflows.
Applied mini-projects tailored to the recruit's incoming department challenges.
Seamless Day Zero corporate transition with zero initial productivity lag.
