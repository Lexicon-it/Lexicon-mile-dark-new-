ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Eligibility Criteria.

Admission is open to domestic candidates fulfilling the following university and state CET guidelines:

01

Should be a citizen of India.

02

Should have passed the 10+2 examination with eligibility as per the admission policy of Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), Pune.

03

Students from any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts) are fully eligible to apply.

04

Should have obtained a non-zero positive score in the Common University Entrance Test (CUET-UG) OR in MAH-BCA/BBA/BMS/BBM CET 2026 conducted by the competent authority.

05

Candidates awaiting final 10+2 examination results may also apply (subject to fulfillment of eligibility criteria at the time of admission).
