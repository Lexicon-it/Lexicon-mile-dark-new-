Partnerships 
At Lexicon MILE, we collaborate with leading universities and academic institutions to enhance the learning experience and bring diverse academic and industry insights into management education. These collaborations help future leaders gain broader exposure, practical knowledge, and a deeper understanding of evolving business environments.
Key Areas of Collaboration 


							Joint Research Initiatives						


						Collaborative research projects with reputed institutions to explore emerging business ideas and industry challenges.
					


							Faculty Exchange & Visiting Experts						


						Interaction with visiting faculty and industry experts to enrich classroom learning with diverse perspectives.
					


							Student Exchange & Learning Opportunities						


						 Opportunities for students to engage in academic experiences beyond the classroom and broaden their learning exposure.					


							Collaborative Curriculum Development						


						Jointly designed modules and electives aligned with evolving business and industry requirements.
					
Our Collaboration 
At Lexicon MILE, partnerships with leading organizations such as eClerx, TIAA, and NISM play an important role in strengthening industry engagement. These collaborations provide future leaders with valuable industry insights, expert interactions, and exposure to real-world business challenges, helping them develop practical skills and strong career readiness.
Our Industry Collaborations
Industry at the Heart of Our Curriculum 
At Lexicon MILE, strong connections with corporates, startups, and professional networks ensure that learning goes beyond the classroom and stays closely aligned with real industry practices.
Masterclasses with CXOs and business leaders for real-world insights.
Internships and live projects with leading companies to gain practical exposure.
Industry-mentored capstone projects focused on solving real business challenges.
Strong placement partnerships across sectors such as FMCG, technology, consulting, and financial services.
Our Recruiting Partners 
Partner with Lexicon MILE. Build the Future of Learning. 
At Lexicon MILE, we welcome collaborations with academic institutions, industry leaders, and organizations who share a vision for meaningful and forward-looking education. Together, we aim to create impactful learning experiences that connect academic knowledge with industry insights and practical application.
