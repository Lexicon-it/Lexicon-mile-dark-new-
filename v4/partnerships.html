Key insights on how Lexicon MILE structures its industry alliances, student benefits, and institutional collaboration frameworks.

Lexicon MILE establishes strategic Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs) with leading corporations across sectors. Collaborations encompass curriculum co-design, CXO masterclasses, joint capstone mentorship, 9-month student internships, executive training, and priority campus hiring drives.

Students gain direct access to industry-recognized capital market certifications (NISM), analytics and digital operations training (eClerx), and global asset management masterclasses (TIAA). These partnerships bridge the gap between academic theory and day-zero corporate execution, giving graduates a competitive edge in campus placements.
