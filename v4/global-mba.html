COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about the MBA Global (USW, UK) with Lexicon MILE:

The Master of Business Administration (MBA Global) is conferred directly by the University of South Wales, United Kingdom. USW is established by UK Royal Charter, accredited through the Privy Council, and monitored by the Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education (QAA). The degree carries full international equivalence and is recognized worldwide across both industry and academia.

The programme comprises 180 total academic credits. Students complete the foundational 60 credits (Terms 1 and 2) at Lexicon MILE Pune covering core managerial disciplines, business analytics, and executive communication. Scholars then transition to the UK for the remaining 120 credits across Terms 3, 4, and 5, culminating in British corporate clinic projects and the work placement.

During the UK residency, students participate in an exclusive 3–4 month corporate work placement arranged in partnership with USW's dedicated Careers and Employability service. Students work within British businesses and international organizations, gaining verifiable UK corporate experience on their CVs and earning stipends based on placement agreements.

Lexicon MILE holds a flawless 100% visa grant track record with 0% rejections across all 7 preceding batches. Our dedicated international desk guides students step-by-step through Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) issuance, financial documentation, biometrics, and immigration compliance.
