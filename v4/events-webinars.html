Events at Lexicon MILE

A Culture of Ideas, Leadership & Engagement

Events at Lexicon MILE play a vital role in creating a vibrant learning ecosystem. From leadership awards and industry conclaves to cultural celebrations and sporting events, these platforms bring together students, industry leaders, and professionals to exchange ideas and celebrate achievements.

Flagship events such as the Bharat Leadership Award, People Power HR Summit, Leadership Conclaves, and International Conferences provide opportunities for meaningful dialogue around leadership, innovation, and business transformation.

These events encourage collaboration, networking, and exposure to diverse perspectives, helping students understand how leadership and business ideas translate into impactful action.

Experimential Immersions

Industrial Visits & Study Tours

Visits to leading organizations allow students to observe operations, understand corporate culture, and connect classroom concepts with industry practices.

TEDx & Thought Leadership Events

TEDx at Lexicon MILE expose students to global ideas, inspiring them to think creatively, challenge assumptions, and share their own perspectives.

Leadership Conclaves & Summits

Conclaves like SheSoars, Entrepreneurship Summits, and HR Forums bring together industry leaders and students to discuss emerging trends, leadership challenges, and future opportunities.

Organizational Study Projects

Students work on real organizational challenges, developing analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, and practical management skills.

Workshops & Social Immersion

Workshops and social initiatives focus on communication, leadership development, and community engagement, helping students build confidence and responsibility as future leaders.

Masterclasses

Masterclasses bring industry expertise into the classroom. Led by experienced professionals and entrepreneurs, these sessions offer practical insights into business trends, leadership, and management practices through interactive discussions and real-world scenarios.

Leadership Talks

Conversations with Leaders Who Shape Industries

Leadership Talks at Lexicon MILE connect future leaders with influential voices from business, entrepreneurship, and public policy. Through interactive sessions, these leaders share their experiences, insights, and perspectives on leadership and strategic decision-making.
