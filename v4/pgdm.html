AI-Integrated PGDM with Global Immersion Program.

The AI-Integrated PGDM with Global Immersion Program at Lexicon MILE, Pune, is a 2-year industry-integrated management programme designed to prepare future leaders for a constantly evolving business environment shaped by technology, innovation, and global change.

Ranked among the leading private B-schools in Pune, the programme blends core management education with emerging domains such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), digital transformation, business analytics, and entrepreneurship.

The Global Immersion experience adds a strong international dimension. Students gain exposure in Malaysia during the first year and in Dubai during the second year, offering insights into diverse business environments and global practices. Students benefit from a powerful mix of academic rigor and experiential learning through leadership conclaves, TEDx events, live industry projects, corporate immersion, and global collaborations.

At the heart of the programme is our unique philosophy — “A Mentor Every MILE”, where each student is guided by industry leaders, experienced faculty, and global practitioners, ensuring continuous mentorship, practical learning, and career readiness as Day Zero Professionals.

Eligibility Criteria.

Bachelor’s Degree

Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree (minimum 3 years duration) in any discipline from a university recognized by the UGC / AIU.

Minimum Qualifying Marks

03

Accepted Entrance Exams

Applicants must possess a valid scorecard in any of the following national tests: CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, MH-CET, ATMA, GMAT, or CUET.

COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about the AI-Integrated PGDM with Global Immersion at Lexicon MILE:

The Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) is an autonomous, industry-driven qualification approved by AICTE. Unlike university MBA programs with rigid theoretical syllabi, our PGDM curriculum is updated annually in direct consultation with top corporate leaders, featuring hands-on corporate immersions in Malaysia and Dubai, executive certifications, and deep integration with the HyperBuild AI Lab.

Yes. The PGDM programme at Lexicon MILE is officially approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), Ministry of Education, Government of India (Approval No. 1-4259511). It is fully recognized across private, public, and multinational corporations worldwide.

The Global Immersion experience is integrated into the two-year academic timeline: Year 1 students undertake an international academic and industrial residency in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur), exploring ASEAN markets and fintech hubs; Year 2 students complete an advanced residency in Dubai (UAE), engaging directly with international business leaders, free-zone executives, and venture capital incubators.

No prior coding or technical background is required. The HyperBuild AI Lab model is designed specifically for business strategists and boardroom executives. Students learn how to apply functional AI tools, prompt engineering, automated workflows, and decision dashboards to solve real marketing, HR, finance, and operational challenges.

Lexicon MILE accepts valid scorecards from CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, MH-CET, ATMA, GMAT, and CUET. Candidates appearing for their final year graduation examinations are also eligible to apply on a provisional basis.

“A Mentor Every MILE” is Lexicon MILE’s signature mentorship ecosystem. From Day 1, every student is paired with both senior academic faculty and veteran corporate mentors from industry-leading companies, ensuring individualized career coaching, networking opportunities, and tailored guidance.
