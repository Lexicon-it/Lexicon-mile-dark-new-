Professional Overview & Analytics Pedagogy

Mr. Deepak Gupta is an Assistant Professor of Analytics at Lexicon MILE Pune. A specialist in quantitative analysis, predictive statistical models, and modern business intelligence tools, he trains future managers to convert complex, multi-dimensional business datasets into actionable corporate strategies.

With more than 11 years of academic mentoring, data science consulting, and corporate training, Mr. Gupta guides scholars in translating algorithmic thinking into commercial boardroom advantage. He serves as an executive mentor within the HYPERBUILD AI Lab, steering real-world data sprints, market forecasting simulations, and data storytelling workshops.

“Data is no longer a technical byproduct; it is the primary compass of modern business. Management leaders must possess the fluency to question algorithms, validate models, and drive data-informed decisions.”

Core Competencies & Technical Frameworks

Mr. Gupta equips students with practical enterprise tools and analytical methodologies required across modern corporate analytics roles:

Predictive Modeling & Forecasting

Regression diagnostics, time-series forecasting, customer lifetime value modeling, and risk scoring for financial and retail institutions.

Business Intelligence (BI) Stacks

Advanced dashboard architecture using Power BI, Tableau, and automated spreadsheet pipelines for executive decision monitoring.

HYPERBUILD AI Lab Sprints

🧠 HyperBuild AI Lab – Dell AI Workstation Pods, managed by Mr. Gupta for data sprint sessions.

Data Storytelling & Strategy

Coaches scholars to translate raw analytical findings into persuasive executive presentations, bridging the communication gap between technical data teams and C-suite leaders.

Course Modules & Enterprise Stack Instruction

Mr. Gupta instructs across core and elective concentrations within the analytics department:

  • Core Courses Delivered: Business Analytics for Decision Making, Predictive Modeling with Python, Advanced Spreadsheet Modeling, Data Visualization & BI Reporting, Operations Research.
  • Enterprise Stack Certification: Leads practical certification labs in Microsoft Power BI, Tableau Desktop, Advanced Excel with Macros, and SQL for Business Analysis.
  • Consulting & Projects: Guides postgraduate capstone projects tackling live datasets from retail supply chains, financial fraud detection, and multi-channel customer churn.
