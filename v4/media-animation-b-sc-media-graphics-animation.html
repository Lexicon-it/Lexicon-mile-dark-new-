Animation Institute in Pune: About Lexicon MILE Department of Media and Animation

Lexicon MILE Department of Media & Animation, formerly Lexicon IMA, has been designed to be the perfect fit for students interested in pursuing careers in Animation, VFX, film-making and related fields. As a leading Animation Institute in Pune, we prepare our students for successful careers in Visual Effects, Graphics, Game Design, Advertising, Marketing, Print, Broadcast, and Digital Journalism. Lexicon IMA is Pune’s only Animation, VFX, and Film-making institute with in-house media powerhouses—Pune Times Mirror and Civic Mirror—offering unmatched industry exposure and real-world creative opportunities!

Certificates, Diplomas & Advanced Diplomas  blends artistic imagination with technical mastery, preparing graduates for careers across animation, VFX, gaming, design, and digital media. With studio-standard infrastructure, AI-driven creative tools, and mentorship from seasoned industry professionals, students graduate with industry-ready portfolios that speak louder than resumes.

At Lexicon Media & Animation, we believe in empowering storytellers and creators for the creator economy. Every student’s canvas becomes their career.

B.Sc. in Media, Graphics & Animation (YCMOU, Nashik) Flagship Degree Program

The BSc in Media & Animation is a comprehensive 3-year undergraduate degree (extendable to 4 years as per NEP) designed for students who want deep, professional-level training in animation, filmmaking, visual effects, game design, graphics, and digital media production.

This program blends academic foundations, studio-driven practicals, high-end software training, industry mentored workshops, and a mandatory 6-month internship, preparing learners to build exceptional portfolios and enter the creative industry with confidence.

Students graduate not just with a degree, but with production-ready skills, an industry-standard showreel, and hands-on experience that employers expect.

What is ImagineX?

A free, on-campus Creative Futures Workshop Series designed by Lexicon MILE

ImagineX is a free, on-campus Creative Futures Workshop Series designed by Lexicon MILE to help school students explore the rapidly growing world of Media, Animation, Design, VFX, UI/UX, Gaming and Generative AI.

 It is a 1-hour, high-impact session conducted by our academic and industry-trained team to help students discover their creative potential and understand modern career opportunities.

Why Schools Need the ImagineX Creative Futures Workshop

The world of careers is changing faster than ever. Today’s students will step into industries driven by digital content, animation, gaming, UI/UX, film, visual effects, virtual production and Generative AI. Yet, most young learners and even parents remain unaware of the creative career opportunities available to them — or the skills these careers demand.

ImagineX bridges this critical awareness gap. (ADD AS A 2 LINE 3×3 GRID WITH ICONS) 

Our workshop helps students:

 • Discover hidden creative talents and visual thinking abilities
 • Gain clarity about modern digital-media professions
 • Understand how animation, graphics, VFX and AI shape the content they consume daily
 • Learn what a portfolio is and why it matters
 • Get exposure to real industry tools and workflows
 • Explore future-ready career options early, even before they pick their streams

The session is crafted to be interactive, inspiring and practical — not theoretical. Students witness live demonstrations, participate in a creative aptitude quiz, and leave with new ideas, aspirations and confidence.
