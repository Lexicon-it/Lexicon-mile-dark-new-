Important Information – MBA Global +
  • The programme fees exclude IELTS, visa, IHS, travel, and UK living expenses.
  • The programme includes 60 credits delivered at Lexicon MILE (Terms 1–2), with progression to University of South Wales (USW) Term 3, subject to meeting eligibility requirements.
  • Admission confirmation is subject to USW eligibility criteria and document verification.
  • All disputes are subject to Pune jurisdiction.

Admission Cancellation & Refund Policy +
  • The Admission Cancellation and Refund Policy will be applicable as per the guidelines of Lexicon MILE and the University of South Wales for the Academic Year 2026–28 MBA Global programme.
  • Requests for admission cancellation must be submitted in writing via email or in person within the prescribed timeline.
  • The institute will strictly follow the cut-off date mentioned in the cancellation and refund policy.
  • If the cancellation request is received before the cut-off date, an amount of ₹1,000 will be deducted.
  • If the cancellation request is received before the cut-off date but after IELTS coaching, ₹21,000 will be deducted.
  • If admission is cancelled after uniform measurement, an additional deduction of ₹15,000 from the registration fee will apply.
  • Refunds related to the University of South Wales (USW) will be processed as per the university’s official refund policy.
