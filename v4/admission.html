CRITERIA · ACADEMICS · ENTRANCE TESTS

Admission Eligibility Benchmarks.

Carefully review the graduation qualification standards, final-year provisional guidelines, and accepted national entrance tests.

PGDM & MBA Global

Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree from a university recognised by UGC / AIU with a minimum aggregate of 50% marks.

Final-year students graduating by June 2027 may apply on a provisional basis.

BBA & HMCT

Candidates must have successfully completed their 10+2 (HSC) or equivalent examination from a recognized State or Central Board.

A minimum aggregate score of 50% is required. Students awaiting final 12th board results are eligible to apply provisionally.

Accepted Entrance Tests (PGDM)

For PGDM and MBA Global programs, applicants must present a valid scorecard from recognized national management aptitude examinations:

ADMISSION PROCESS · ALL PROGRAMMES

How to Apply in 3 Simple Phases.

The application process consists of three simple phases, and applicants must complete all three steps to qualify for the selection process. A single common online window facilitates applications for all programs including MBA Global, PGDM, BBA & HMCT.

Applicable to MBA Global (USW, UK) Applicable to PGDM (All Specializations) Application Entrance Fee: ₹1,000 Est. Completion Time: 15–20 Mins

This is the first step of your application. Fill out the common application form by providing your personal details and preferred programme.

Personal & Contact Details

Enter your full legal name matching your 10th marksheet or valid Passport, active mobile number (verified via instant OTP), and primary email address.

Programme Preference Selection

Select your intended qualification: MBA Global (University of South Wales, UK) or PGDM (Marketing & Finance / Research & Business Analytics / Business Management).

Application ID Generation

Upon registration, a unique applicant credentials ID is instantly created for your personal candidate dashboard, tracking your progress throughout admissions.

  • Pro Tip for MBA Global Applicants: Ensure candidate name matches exactly with your Valid International Passport for subsequent UK CAS and Student Visa processing.
  • No document upload is required in Phase 1; profile registration takes under 3 minutes.

You are required to make a non-refundable payment of INR 1000 towards the admission process entrance fee online. Upon successful payment, you will be redirected to the next phase of the application, where you need to submit the required credentials to complete your application.

Secure Payment Modes

Integrated payment gateway supporting UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 50+ banks, Debit Cards, and Credit Cards.

Instant Payment Receipt

An official digital invoice and payment confirmation voucher is emailed and stored inside your portal immediately after checkout.

Automated Phase 3 Unlocking

Zero processing delay. Once your INR 1,000 transaction succeeds, your application file immediately unlocks Phase 3 for credential submission.

  • Fee is universally uniform across both PGDM and MBA Global candidate registrations.
  • 100% encrypted 256-bit SSL transaction gateway. Non-refundable processing fee.

Provide the following details to complete your application and qualify for the final personal interview and selection rounds:

Academic Details

Class 10th & 12th percentages/grades, Graduation degree stream, college name, and aggregate percentage. Final-year candidates can provide marks up to their latest completed semester.

Extracurricular & Work Experience

Details of sports, arts, leadership initiatives, internships, or full-time corporate work experience (if any; freshers are equally encouraged to apply).

Aptitude Test Score

Scorecard and roll number for accepted national exams: CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT, MH-CET, or ATMA. Candidates awaiting exam results can register roll numbers.

Address for Correspondence

Permanent and current correspondence postal address with accurate PIN code for official hardcopy offer letters and formal documentation.

Preferred Interview Venue

Select your preferred personal interview venue from nationwide physical center locations (Pune Campus, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Patna, Ranchi, Jaipur, Indore, Lucknow) or choose Online Video Interview.

  • Submission of Phase 3 confirms your seat in the upcoming personal evaluation and PI interview slot.
  • Interview schedule confirmation with date, panel link/venue details will be sent via email within 48 hours.

Documents to Keep Ready Before Starting

Keep digital copies of these essential records handy for smooth Phase 3 submission:

Recent Color Photograph 10th & 12th Marksheets Graduation Marksheets / Degree National Test Scorecard (CAT/XAT/CMAT/MAT/GMAT/CET) Valid Passport (Mandatory for MBA Global UK Route)
