PGDM/MBA Global ADMISSION

Ready to prepare for professional life, accelerate your career, and explore new frontiers of knowledge? Be limitless. Join a community of driven and talented students as you pursue your management career.

Lexicon MILE, Pune, is more than just a business school. It is a platform to explore, discover, learn, and grow every day. Here, you will be challenged, supported, and inspired to achieve your best.

Our PGDM / MBA Global admission process is designed to be simple and accessible through a common online application window, allowing you to apply for your preferred programme.

On this page, you will find all the details related to how to apply, eligibility criteria, scholarships, fees, and the overall selection process. Choose your programme and get started.

Follow the steps below to complete your admission application. To begin, register online, and one of our admissions counsellors will connect with you to guide you through the entire process.

Eligibility

Image Placeholder

Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks or an equivalent CGPA. Students currently in the final year of graduation, expected to complete their degree by June 30, 2026, are also eligible to apply.

For such candidates, admission will be provisional, and they must submit proof of graduation completion by October 1, 2026, to continue in the program. Failure to submit the required documentation may lead to cancellation of admission.

Applicants must also have a valid aptitude test score from any of the following: CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT, MH-CET or ATMA.

How to Apply

The application process consists of three simple phases, and applicants must complete all three steps to qualify for the selection process.

Register Your Profile

This is the first step of your application. Fill out the common application form by providing your personal details and preferred programme.

Pay Your Admission Process Entrance Fee

You are required to make a non-refundable payment of INR 1000 towards the admission process entrance fee online. Upon successful payment, you will be redirected to the next phase of the application, where you need to submit the required credentials to complete your application.

Complete Your Application

Provide the following details to complete your application:

  • Academic details

  • Extracurricular achievements and work experience (if any)

  • Aptitude test score (CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, GMAT, MH-CET, or ATMA)

  • Address for correspondence
  • Preferred interview venue

Scholarship

p>At Lexicon MILE, we value exceptional talent and aim to reduce financial barriers for deserving students. Through our scholarship program, we offer a limited number of scholarships on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to admission confirmation and payment of the first installment of fees.
