CRITERIA · ACADEMICS · ENTRANCE TESTS

Admission Eligibility Benchmarks.

Carefully review the graduation qualification standards, final-year provisional guidelines, and accepted national entrance tests.

🎓

Graduation Benchmark

Applicants must hold a Bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a university recognised by the UGC / Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Minimum aggregate score required is 50% marks (or equivalent CGPA). Relaxation as per regulatory guidelines applies for reserved categories.

📅

Final Year Graduates

Students currently in the final year of graduation who are scheduled to complete all degree requirements by June 30, 2026, are eligible to apply.

Admission for such candidates will remain provisional. Official proof of graduation completion must be submitted by October 1, 2026.

📊

Accepted Entrance Tests

Applicants must present a valid scorecard from any of the recognized national management aptitude examinations:

CAT XAT CMAT MAT GMAT MH-CET ATMA

STEP-BY-STEP ADMISSION ROADMAP

How to Apply in 3 Simple Phases.

The entire application process is conducted through a single common online window. Complete all three phases to confirm your eligibility for selection rounds.

Phase 01 Step 1 of 3

Register Your Profile

Initiate your application on the official admissions portal by entering your basic personal profile coordinates and program preference.

  • Full candidate name, email & mobile verification
  • Choice of preferred programme (PGDM or MBA Global)
  • Instant generation of unique Application ID
Phase 02 ₹1,000 Application Fee

Pay Entrance Processing Fee

Pay the non-refundable entrance application fee of INR 1,000 online using the secure payment gateway to activate your full candidate file.

  • Secure checkout via Net Banking, UPI, Cards
  • Instant digital payment receipt generation
  • Unlocks Phase 3 document & score submission
Phase 03 Final Step

Complete Credentials & Slot Selection

Submit your academic background, test percentiles, and work history, then book your personal interview venue or online schedule.

  • 10th, 12th & Graduation semester marks
  • Aptitude test score (CAT, XAT, CMAT, MAT, etc.)
  • Work experience, achievements & interview venue
