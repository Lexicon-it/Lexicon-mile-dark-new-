FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About the Program.

Find answers regarding degree accreditation, visa regulations, technical prerequisites, and international placement assistance.

What is the difference between a traditional MBA and an MBA in Business Analytics? +
A traditional MBA focuses primarily on general management theories such as marketing principles, operational administration, and human resources. In contrast, the MBA in Business Analytics equips leaders with empirical computational decisioning: predictive modeling, artificial intelligence toolstacks, data warehousing, big data architectures, and quantitative econometric modeling, ensuring every strategic decision is supported by data rather than guesswork.
Is the MBA degree awarded by INTI International University recognized in India and globally? +
Yes, absolutely. INTI International University is recognized by the Ministry of Higher Education (MOHE) Malaysia, fully accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA), and ranked among the Top 300 universities worldwide for Business & Management Studies in the QS World Rankings. In India, foreign university degrees from accredited institutions are recognized for corporate recruitments and multinational corporate careers worldwide.
Do I need prior coding or engineering knowledge to enroll? +
No technical background is mandatory. The program is carefully architected with foundational modules at Lexicon MILE (Pune) covering statistics, Python, and low-code AI platforms from the ground up. Whether you come from a Commerce, Arts, Science, or Engineering background, our faculty and HyperBuild AI Lab mentors guide you step-by-step through practical fluency.
How does the HyperBuild AI Lab support students during the course? +
The HyperBuild AI Lab is Lexicon MILE's state-of-the-art center of excellence for applied artificial intelligence. Students work directly on live corporate data, build automated machine learning pipelines, master no-code analytics frameworks, and interact with CXOs from leading tech firms, ensuring they do not just learn about analytics, but actively build enterprise solutions with it.
How is the Malaysian Student Visa (EMGS) process handled? +
Lexicon MILE and INTI International University provide dedicated, end-to-end institutional visa support. The university handles your Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) submission, student pass approvals, and health screening protocols, ensuring a seamless immigration transition to Malaysia.
What career and placement assistance is offered to graduates? +
Students receive comprehensive 100% placement support from both Lexicon MILE's robust 500+ corporate recruiter network in India and INTI's 1,000+ multinational corporate network across Southeast Asia. Roles range from Analytics Consultant, Growth Manager, Risk Modeler, to BI Lead with competitive international packages.
What accommodation and student living arrangements exist at INTI Malaysia? +
INTI's 82-acre Nilai campus boasts modern, air-conditioned on-campus student residences with high-speed Wi-Fi, 24/7 security, multi-cuisine cafeterias, an Olympic-sized swimming pool, gymnasiums, and convenient shuttle transportation connecting to Kuala Lumpur city center.
