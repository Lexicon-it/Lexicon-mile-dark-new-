FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know About the Program.

Find clear answers regarding degree accreditation, Swiss partnership, eligibility, and dual-country immersion.

1. What is an MBA in Family Business Management? +
An MBA in Family Business Management is a specialized postgraduate program designed to address the unique challenges of family-owned and legacy businesses. It focuses on succession planning, family governance, leadership development, conflict management, and long-term business sustainability.
2. How is this MBA different from a regular MBA? +
Unlike a traditional MBA that prepares students for corporate roles, this program is tailored for individuals who will own, inherit, or lead family enterprises. It emphasizes ownership structures, generational transitions, governance frameworks, and real-world family business scenarios.
3. Who should pursue an MBA in Family Business Management? +
This program is ideal for heirs and successors of family businesses, current family business owners and managers, entrepreneurs from business families, and professionals planning careers in family business consulting or advisory roles.
4. Does the program include international exposure? +
Yes. The MBA is offered in partnership with SBS Swiss Business School (RAK, UAE) and includes international academic exposure aligned with global business and governance practices, giving students a strong global perspective.
5. What career opportunities are available after completing this MBA? +
Graduates are prepared for top leadership roles: Next-generation family business leader or successor, strategic business or unit head, entrepreneur or new venture founder, and family office, wealth management, or operations manager.
6. Is work experience mandatory to apply for this program? +
Work experience is preferred but not mandatory. Candidates with family business involvement or entrepreneurial exposure are encouraged to apply, even if they have limited formal corporate work experience.
7. What are the eligibility criteria for admission? +
Applicants must hold a bachelor’s degree from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% marks. A minimum of two years of work experience is preferred but not compulsory.
8. Where will the program be conducted? +
The program follows a dual-location learning model, with specialized modules delivered at Lexicon MILE, Pune (India), and core international management modules at SBS Swiss Business School (RAK, UAE).
9. Is this MBA recognized internationally? +
Yes. The curriculum is globally benchmarked and aligned with international accreditation standards such as IACBE and ACBSP, ensuring global academic credibility and worldwide recognition.
10. How do I apply for the MBA in Family Business Management? +
Interested candidates can apply online directly through the official Lexicon MILE website or through the Google application form. Admissions are offered on a limited-seat basis and are subject to eligibility criteria and personal interviews.
