Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the total fee for PGDM at Lexicon MILE Pune?

The total tuition fee for the AI-Integrated PGDM program at Lexicon MILE ranges between ₹10.8 lakh, depending on specialization and additional components.

What is the fee structure for MBA Global at Lexicon MILE?

The MBA Global program at Lexicon MILE follows a 60 + 120 credit international pathway in academic partnership with the University of South Wales (UK).
The fee structure is as follows:
• Registration Fee: ₹1,50,000
• Lexicon MILE Fee: ₹7,50,000 (including registration fee)
• University of South Wales (UK) Fee: GBP 7,500 (approx.)
The program includes 60 credits at Lexicon MILE, Pune, and 120 credits at the University of South Wales, UK. The University of South Wales awards the degree upon successful completion.

What entrance exams are accepted for Lexicon MILE PGDM admission?

Lexicon MILE accepts national-level entrance exams, including CAT, XAT, MAT, CMAT, MH-CET, ATMA, and GMAT.

What are the eligibility criteria for PGDM admission at Lexicon MILE?

Candidates must have a minimum of 50% marks in Graduation from a recognized university along with a valid entrance exam score. Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview.

Does Lexicon MILE offer good placement packages?

Yes, Lexicon MILE has recorded strong placement outcomes, with the highest package going up to ₹49 LPA for MBA Global and ₹22 LPA for PGDM.

Is there a Personal Interview for Lexicon MILE admission?

Yes, shortlisted candidates are required to appear for a Personal Interview as part of the final selection process.

How can I apply for Lexicon MILE PGDM or MBA Global?

Candidates can apply online through the official Lexicon MILE website by filling out the application form and submitting the required documents.

Are admissions open for the MBA Global at Lexicon MILE?

Yes, admissions are currently open for the MBA Global program offered in academic partnership with the University of South Wales (UK).

Does Lexicon MILE provide placement support?

Are there multiple placement opportunities at Lexicon MILE?

Yes, Lexicon MILE offers multiple placement opportunities supported by a strong returning recruiter network and corporate partnerships.

Does Lexicon MILE offer scholarships for PGDM students?

Yes, Lexicon MILE offers merit-based and need-based scholarships for eligible PGDM students based on entrance exam performance.

What is the scholarship amount for CAT students at Lexicon MILE?

Students scoring 80 percentile and above in CAT are eligible for a scholarship of ₹1,00,000.

What scholarship is available for CMAT and MAT scores?

CMAT candidates with 90 percentile and above can receive ₹75,000, while MAT candidates with 90 percentile and above can receive ₹50,000.
